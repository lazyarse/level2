Security Cam turns an unused Android phone into a security camera. It watches
the camera and microphone for motion, people, faces, pets, vehicles, loud
sounds and more — entirely on-device — and alerts you through channels you
configure yourself (app log, email via your own SMTP server, Telegram bot,
Pushover, or webhook presets including self-hostable ntfy).

- No account, no cloud, no tracking: detection runs locally with on-device
  ML models; network is used only for alerts you explicitly configure.
- Video clips are recorded around every trigger (5 s pre-roll + 5 s
  post-roll) and stored on the device.
- Monitoring runs as a foreground service, so it survives screen-off.
- Face recognition (known/unknown faces) is available in the full build;
  the F-Droid build excludes the face-embedding model and its UI while
  keeping plain face detection.
